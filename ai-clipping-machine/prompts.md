# Prompts

Copy, paste into Claude, and swap the parts in `< >`.

**Clip a video**
```text
Clip this video: <YOUR YOUTUBE LINK>
```

**Schedule with Zernio**
```text
Schedule reels 1, 2 and 4 on my Instagram, one a day, starting today. Ask me to confirm before you post anything.
```

**Write captions**
```text
From now on, write an Instagram caption for every reel: a hook, one line of value and a call to action. Show me before you schedule.
```

**Weekly routine**
```text
Once a week, check my YouTube channel for new videos: <YOUR CHANNEL LINK>. When there's a new one, clip it, build the review page and ping me here. Don't schedule anything until I approve.
```

**Change the look**
```text
Make the captions yellow and use a different font.
```
